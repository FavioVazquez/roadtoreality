# Prior art (Haiku researcher, 13 Oct 2026; saved by Opus, condensed; citation counts unavailable, APIs rate-limited)
1 Tycho/Kepler from raw journals: PARTLY.
  - Li, Ji & Zhang?, "From Kepler to Newton: Explainable AI for Science", arXiv:2111.12210: uses "only Tycho's Mars observations" to recover Kepler's and Newton's laws. CLOSEST. READ (Opus, 13 Oct via ar5iv): Li, Ji, Zhang, ICML-AI4Science 2022; data = 28 rows of Kepler's REDUCED heliocentric longitudes and distances from Astronomia Nova (Donahue transl.), 25 train / 3 validation; no raw journals. Ours differs: raw instrument readings from Dreyer, reduced night by night, checked against DE441.
  - KeplerBench (anonymous NeurIPS 2026 submission under review, OpenReview RdErwlc7jJ; PDF read 14 Oct, kept private, "do not distribute"; owner 14 Oct: cite the public OpenReview record only): CLOSEST for ep1. Pilot: Tycho's Mars DECLINATIONS from Pafko's 2000 transcription of Dreyer, 354 nightly epochs 1582-1600 (268 train pre-1594, 86 held out); an ellipse with MODERN Earth orbit supplied, 6 params, median |res| 2.66' in sample, 13.31' held out; "oracle" (modern orbits) = Tycho's error; noise floor ~2'. Ours differs: raw instrument readings reduced night by night (sextant distances, armillary, quadrant altitudes, clock times) to longitude AND latitude with per-session errors, verified against DE441; Earth's orbit fitted from the same data; circle+equant vs ellipse (8') test. Novelty wording: an ellipse HAS been fitted to Tycho's Mars record (KeplerBench pilot, from Pafko's declinations); we could not find a reduction of the raw readings.
  - Pafko (pafko.com/tycho): ~800 Mars entries from Opera Omnia in Excel, visualization only, no fit, no page refs.
  - Krisciunas arXiv:1610.05750 (own 75 naked-eye obs). Gingerich & Voelkel JHA 1998; Gingerich Physics Today 2011; Wesley JHA 1978 (accuracy from logs); Schou 2025 JHA (mural quadrant); Carman & Recio 2022.
  - Night-by-night Tycho Mars vs modern ephemeris: not found.
2 Mercury from historical transits: PARTLY. CLOSEST: Morrison & Ward 1975 (MNRAS 173, 183): ~2400 internal-contact times 1677-1973 fitted with the analytical theories of their day, excess perihelion motion +41.9 ± 0.5"/cy; ours (refit of Newcomb's 1882 set with a modern N-body model, 43.71 ± 1.04) is an independent check against theirs. Also Constantin 2011 (Le Verrier refit 42.8), Duncombe 1956 (43.11 ± 0.45). Roseveare 1982 (OUP) history; Le Verrier 1859 38.3"; Newcomb 1882. The 1677 St Helena sign slip is Newcomb's OWN corrigendum (printed p. 484: "for +29s.2 read -25s.1"); our kill test independently recovered it, no claim of a new error.

- KeplerBench PUBLIC abstract (author pasted it from OpenReview RdErwlc7jJ, 14 Oct; workshop-track submission no. 199): benchmark for law discovery from observer-centred astronomical measurements without privileged variables, historical (Tycho) plus synthetic star systems, three scored levels (prediction, global law, local law). Its pilot on Tycho's Mars record reports that a flexible interpolator fits the observations and fails on later years while a Keplerian orbit degrades gracefully: QUALITATIVELY THE SAME as our ep1 agnostic held-out result. Novelty for ep1 must therefore rest on the raw-reading reduction, Earth fitted from the same record, agent-chosen families under pre-registration, the disguised runs and the independent checks, not on 'flexible fails, Kepler predicts'.
